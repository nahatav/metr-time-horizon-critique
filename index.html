S1

Log scale makes doubling a straight line

Steady growth reads as a steady slope, through position on a common scale, the most accurate channel (L4). Ratios also fit Weber’s law (L4).

W1

Uncertainty is drawn at 20% opacity

Opus 4.6 reads as 12 h, yet its 95% CI spans 5 to 61 h. Faint whiskers sink into the ground (L5). Fix: solid CI bars.

S2

Example tasks give the axis meaning

“Train classifier” at about an hour turns minutes into kinds of work, a conceptual unit (data models, L2).

W2

One straight line hides a bend

Every frontier model since o3 sits above METR’s line. Continuity (L5) reads it as one pace. Fix: add the recent fit.

S3

It marks where its data stops

Hatching and the gray Mythos dot pop out preattentively (L4), so the chart claims nothing its tasks cannot measure (expressiveness, L2).

W3

“50%” carries the headline

At 80% success Opus 4.6 drops from 12 h to 70 min, yet the threshold sits only in the title and a rotated label (L4). Fix: plot both.

W4

Light and split dots go unexplained

No key for light green, 10 of 26 models named, two models share one split dot (expressiveness, L2). Fix: key and jitter.
